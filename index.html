How to Read These Prices

  • Sale Price (*) is the vehicle’s MSRP less the Bob Weaver dealer discount and any manufacturer offer every buyer receives — rebates, customer cash, bonus cash and purchase allowances. Every buyer gets this price, with no conditions to meet.
  • Your Best Price (†) additionally subtracts manufacturer offers that not every buyer will qualify for — each one is itemized on the card so you can see exactly which apply to you. Qualification is determined at the time of sale.
  • Conditional offers shown are trade-in allowances (which require you to trade a qualifying vehicle), Stellantis lease-loyalty bonus cash (which requires a qualifying current lease and financing through Stellantis Financial Services), and the manufacturer military, first responder and educator programs (which require proof of eligibility). Qualification is determined at the time of sale — ask us which apply to you.

Financing & Lease Offers

  • Advertised APR offers are available only to well-qualified buyers who finance through the manufacturer’s captive lender (GM Financial or Stellantis Financial), subject to credit approval. Not all buyers will qualify.
  • Special financing rates generally cannot be combined with manufacturer cash offers. Where a card shows both an APR offer and a cash savings figure, they are alternatives — you choose one. The sale price shown reflects taking the cash, so choosing the advertised APR instead will change the price. Ask us to run both so you can see which saves you more.
  • The Ram 1500 12% off MSRP rebate is a standalone offer. It cannot be combined with retail bonus cash — a buyer takes one or the other, and the prices shown reflect the 12%. The same is true of the 10% below MSRP rebate on the Grand Cherokee.

Vehicle Availability

  • Every offer on this page applies to one specific vehicle, identified by stock number, and is subject to prior sale on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Vehicles flagged as a demo or courtesy transportation unit have been driven and carry the mileage shown on the card. They are sold as new vehicles.
  • Photographs are of the actual vehicle where available. Feature descriptions are for reference and may not reflect every option on a specific unit.
  • Dealer is not responsible for typographical, photographic, or pricing errors. All offers subject to correction.
