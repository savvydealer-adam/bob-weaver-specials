Pricing Structure

  • Each vehicle displays two pricing tiers. The headline “everyone” payment is available to all qualified buyers with approved credit through GM Financial. No loyalty, conquest, or rewards-card enrollment is required for the headline price.
  • Where shown, “Current Lessees” pricing requires specific loyalty or conquest eligibility as described on each vehicle card. Not all buyers will qualify for the lower conditional payment.
  • “You May Also Qualify For” items (such as My GM Rewards Card Sign-Up Save) require additional enrollment or actions and are not included in the headline payment.

Lease Terms & Payments

  • All advertised lease offers are based on closed-end leases through GM Financial with approved above-average credit (typically Tier 1 or Tier 2).
  • All advertised monthly payments and due-at-signing amounts include all applicable Pennsylvania taxes and fees (sales tax, title, license, government fees, and dealer processing/documentation fees).
  • No security deposit required on featured lease vehicles.
  • All leases are 36 months at 10,000 miles per year unless otherwise noted. The GMC Canyon (Stock #G26570) lease is 39 months.
  • Total of payments shown reflects the base monthly payment multiplied by the lease term and does not include the amount due at signing.

Finance Terms

  • The 2026 Chevrolet Trax (Stock #C26630) is a finance offer, not a lease. Finance terms: 5.9% APR for 84 months through GM Financial with approved credit and $1,000 due at signing. All PA taxes and fees are included in the advertised payment.
  • Headline payment of $371/mo reflects selling price of $25,063 (includes $1,250 rebate and $750 bonus cash). My GM Rewards Card Sign-Up Save ($500) requires new enrollment and is not included in the headline payment.
  • Monthly payment and APR may vary based on individual creditworthiness and lender approval.

Purchase Option

  • 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 (Stock #C26660) is available for purchase in addition to the advertised lease: 0% APR for 60 months or 1.9% APR for 72 months on approved credit through GM Financial.
  • Total purchase savings of up to $11,850 include: $4,350 Bob Weaver Discount, $4,250 Consumer Cash, $1,000 Trade Assist (requires trade-in of 2012 model year or newer vehicle), $1,750 Bonus Cash, and $500 My GM Rewards Card Sign-Up Save.
  • Not all buyers will qualify for all incentives. Trade Assist requires a qualifying trade-in vehicle. See dealer for complete details.

Credit & Qualification

  • All offers require approved above-average credit through GM Financial. Not all applicants will qualify for the lowest payment or best terms shown.
  • Monthly payment, down payment, and lease/finance terms may vary based on individual creditworthiness and lender approval.
  • Lender may require additional down payment, adjusted terms, or security deposit based on credit review.
  • Offers are subject to lender program availability and may be withdrawn at any time without notice.

Courtesy Transportation Program (CTP) Vehicles

  • Several vehicles shown are Courtesy Transportation Program (CTP) vehicles previously used in dealership service operations. These vehicles have existing mileage as noted on each vehicle listing.
  • CTP vehicles are titled as new but have been driven and may show minor wear consistent with their use and mileage.
  • For leased CTP vehicles, the existing mileage counts toward the total mileage allowance. The effective remaining mileage allowance per year may be lower than the stated 10,000 miles/year after accounting for odometer mileage at lease signing.
  • **2026 Chevrolet Blazer EV (Stock #C26582): This vehicle has approximately 2,273 miles on the odometer. The 36-month lease allowance is 30,000 total miles (10,000/yr); after accounting for existing mileage, the effective remaining allowance is approximately 9,200 miles per year.

Mileage & Wear

  • All featured leases allow 10,000 miles per year unless otherwise noted (see CTP vehicle section above for adjusted allowances).
  • Lessee is responsible for excess mileage charges of $0.25 per mile over the allowed mileage at lease end.
  • Lessee is responsible for excessive wear and use charges as defined by the lessor’s published wear and use standards, which will be provided at lease signing.
  • Higher annual mileage options (12,000 or 15,000 miles/year) may be available at additional monthly cost. See dealer for pricing.

Incentives & Rebates

  • Customer Cash Rebates included in advertised payments where noted are manufacturer incentives and are subject to change or cancellation by the manufacturer without notice.
  • GM Lease Loyalty: Requires lessee to have a current GM lease through GM Financial or Ally. Proof of current lease contract required at time of signing. Specific brand loyalty (e.g., Chevrolet Lease Loyalty) may require a current lease of that specific brand. Loyalty incentives are not included in the headline “everyone” payment and are reflected only in the “Current Lessees” tier.
  • Lease Conquest: Requires lessee to have a current lease of a competitive (non-GM) brand vehicle. Proof of current competitive brand lease required at time of signing. Conquest incentives are not included in the headline “everyone” payment.
  • My GM Rewards Card Sign-Up Save ($500): Requires new enrollment in the My GM Rewards Card program. Subject to credit approval for the rewards card. This incentive is not included in the headline “everyone” payment and is shown separately under “You May Also Qualify For” or included in the “Current Lessees” tier where applicable. See dealer or gmrewardscard.com for complete terms and conditions.
  • GMC Truck Month Bonus: Available on qualifying GMC trucks during the promotional period. Subject to availability and may be withdrawn by the manufacturer without notice.
  • Incentives and rebates may not be combined with certain other manufacturer or dealer offers. See dealer for offer compatibility.

End of Lease

  • At lease end, lessee has the option to: (a) return the vehicle and pay any end-of-lease charges, (b) purchase the vehicle at the predetermined residual value established at lease signing plus any applicable fees and taxes, or (c) lease or purchase a new vehicle.
  • Early termination of the lease may result in substantial early termination charges as outlined in the lease agreement, including remaining depreciation and finance charges.
  • A disposition fee (typically $395–$595) may apply at lease end if the vehicle is returned and the lessee does not lease or purchase a new GM vehicle.
  • Lessee is responsible for any excess wear and use charges and over-mileage charges assessed at lease return.

Insurance & Requirements

  • Lessee must maintain comprehensive and collision insurance coverage on the leased vehicle for the entire duration of the lease, with the lessor named as loss payee and additional insured. Minimum coverage limits are determined by the lessor.
  • Lessee is responsible for all scheduled maintenance, repairs, and service during the lease term in accordance with the manufacturer’s recommended maintenance schedule.
  • GAP (Guaranteed Asset Protection) coverage may be included with the lease or available for purchase. GAP coverage protects the lessee in the event the vehicle is totaled or stolen and the insurance settlement is less than the remaining lease balance. See your lease agreement or dealer for details.
