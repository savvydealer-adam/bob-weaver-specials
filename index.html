How to Read These Prices

  • Sale Price (*) is the vehicle’s MSRP less the Bob Weaver dealer discount. Every buyer gets this price, with no conditions to meet.
  • Your Best Price (†) additionally subtracts manufacturer offers that not every buyer will qualify for — each one is itemized on the card so you can see exactly which apply to you. Qualification is determined at the time of sale.
  • Conditional offers shown may include consumer cash, bonus cash, trade-in allowances (which require you to trade a qualifying 2012-or-newer vehicle), and GM Rewards or My Rewards sign-up and spend bonuses (which require enrolling in the program and redeeming earned points).

Financing & Lease Offers

  • Advertised APR offers — 0% for 60 months, 2.9% for 72 months, 4.9% for 72 months — are available only to well-qualified buyers who finance through the manufacturer’s captive lender (GM Financial or Stellantis Financial), subject to credit approval. Not all buyers will qualify.
  • Special financing rates generally cannot be combined with manufacturer cash offers. Where a card shows both an APR offer and a cash savings figure, they are alternatives — you choose one. Ask us to run both so you can see which saves you more.
  • Advertised lease payments are 36-month, 12,000-mile-per-year leases with the stated amount due at signing, and include Pennsylvania tax and fees. Payments are estimates based on current program terms and are subject to credit approval and change without notice.
  • Advertised finance payments on the Equinox and Trax are 84-month terms with $1,000 down at an estimated 6.25% APR and include Pennsylvania tax and fees. Your actual rate, term and payment depend on your credit and are determined at the time of sale.

Vehicle Availability

  • Every offer on this page applies to one specific vehicle, identified by stock number, and is subject to prior sale on a first-come, first-served basis.
  • Vehicles flagged as a demo or courtesy transportation unit have been driven and carry the mileage shown on the card. They are sold as new vehicles.
  • Photographs are of the actual vehicle where available. Feature descriptions are for reference and may not reflect every option on a specific unit.
  • Dealer is not responsible for typographical, photographic, or pricing errors. All offers subject to correction.
